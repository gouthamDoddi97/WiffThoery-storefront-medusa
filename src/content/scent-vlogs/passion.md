# The making of Passion

Passion started as a dare: could a saffron opening feel warm instead of medicinal? Three trial batches say yes — eventually.

## Lab log

- DAY 01 — First blend. Saffron overdosed; smelled like a spice rack.
- DAY 09 — Trial 2. Rose absolute anchored the heart, oud pulled it down a register.
- DAY 21 — Maceration complete. Amber and vanilla hum arrives around hour three.
- DAY 24 — Wear test on fabric: 8+ hours, projection strongest in the first two.
